Gemini is Google's assistant on the web and in the Gemini app. With [Connected Apps](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13695044?hl=en), it can use other apps to complete a request and, with your permission, take actions in those apps.

**Runtime:** The web app at gemini.google.com and the Gemini mobile apps. Calls, messages, and device settings in the help center are described for Android. Which apps show up depends on the Gemini surface, device, account, and country.

**Acts on:** Apps you connect. Google's help center gives examples such as summarizing Gmail, creating calendar events, playing music, shopping or booking through available apps, messaging on Android, and controlling Google Home devices. On the web you can name an app with @. Each app's settings page lists the actions it supports.

**Extensions:** Connected Apps. In the web app those settings are labeled Connectors. You choose what stays connected, and some apps connect only after you approve them. This list does not catalog individual apps.

**Notes:** Connected Apps require a signed-in Gemini account. If Keep Activity is off, they are unavailable on gemini.google.com, on iOS, and on watches; on Android only Device assistance, Phone, Messages, and WhatsApp remain. This entry is the Gemini assistant. Gemini CLI is a coding interface and belongs in [Awesome-Vibe-Coding](https://github.com/0xWelt/Awesome-Vibe-Coding).
