ChatGPT is OpenAI's assistant for questions and, on paid plans, for tasks that use your apps and the web. The action path documented now is [ChatGPT Work](https://openai.com/chatgpt-work/), including a [cloud browser](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001280-using-cloud-browser-in-chatgpt). [Operator](https://openai.com/index/introducing-operator/), the earlier research-preview browser agent, was brought into ChatGPT as agent mode. OpenAI's [agent-mode article](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11752874-chatgpt-agent) now says that mode is no longer available and points people to ChatGPT Work.

**Runtime:** ChatGPT on the web and in its apps. Cloud browser is a browser on a separate computer in the cloud. A task can start on web or mobile and keep running after you leave, until it needs input, sign-in, or confirmation. The ChatGPT desktop app also has a built-in browser for following a task on your machine.

**Acts on:** Public and signed-in websites, after you approve site access and complete sign-in in a secure form. ChatGPT asks before opening a new site in the cloud browser, and asks again before an action that would be hard to reverse or that creates a financial, legal, account, or other real-world commitment, such as a booking or a payment. ChatGPT Work can schedule one-time or recurring tasks, including shared team tasks that use approved company tools.

**Extensions:** [Connected apps](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11487775-connected-apps-in-chatgpt) and plugins. Apps connect ChatGPT to services you already use, such as Google Drive or Slack, so it can find or summarize information and, depending on the app, take actions. When an app or plugin can do the task directly, ChatGPT may use that path and skip the browser.

**Notes:** Cloud browser ships in ChatGPT Work on paid plans in supported regions, excluding Free and Go, and can depend on workspace permissions. This entry is the personal and work assistant.
