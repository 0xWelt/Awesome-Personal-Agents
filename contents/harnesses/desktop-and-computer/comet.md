Comet is Perplexity's browser with a built-in assistant, for people who want the browser to take on tasks while they browse.

**Runtime:** Apps for Mac, Windows, iOS, and Android, as listed on the [Comet product page](https://www.perplexity.ai/comet).

**Acts on:** The product page describes delegated tasks such as inbox work, shopping, finances, and trip planning, including drafting a reply that shares an upcoming schedule.

**Extensions:** The assistant acts inside the browser, across the session you are already in. Public Comet pages reviewed for this entry do not describe a separate connector or skill catalog.

**Notes:** Comet is the browser agent. The Perplexity answer app is a separate search product. The product page describes these jobs at a high level and does not publish a full matrix of which steps wait for confirmation.
