Claude is Anthropic's assistant. [Claude Cowork](https://claude.com/product/cowork) is where you hand it real work: multi-step tasks in the files and tools you choose, returned for review.

**Runtime:** Web, desktop, and mobile. Every conversation starts as chat, and Cowork's capabilities are used when the task needs them. You can schedule a task on a cadence and it keeps running after the laptop is closed. A task can also be started from the phone and continued in the cloud; the product page lists that phone flow on Pro, Max, and Ultra.

**Acts on:** Files and folders you allow, connected tools, and sites opened in Cowork's browser, including reading a page or filling a form. The product page describes finished documents, spreadsheets, and slide decks, and projects split into chunks that run together.

**Extensions:** Connectors, plugins, skills, and sub-agents. Plugins bundle tools, knowledge, and workflows into one install. Computer use, in research preview, can open apps and operate the screen. Cowork tries connectors first, then the browser, and uses the screen last. Screen access is designed to ask permission before each application, and you can stop it.

**Notes:** Claude Code is Anthropic's separate product for writing and shipping software, and it is tracked in [Awesome-Vibe-Coding](https://github.com/0xWelt/Awesome-Vibe-Coding). Cowork is the surface for non-coding knowledge work. The product page says the unified chat-and-Cowork experience is still rolling out; until it reaches an account, Cowork appears next to Chat.
